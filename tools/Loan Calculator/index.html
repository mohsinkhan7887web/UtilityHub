How is a loan payment calculated?

For a fixed-rate loan with monthly payments, the payment depends on the principal, monthly interest rate, and number of payments.

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Here, P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments. At 0% interest, the loan amount is divided equally across the payments.
