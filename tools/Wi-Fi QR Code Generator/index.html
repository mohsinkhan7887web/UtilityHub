Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool connect me to Wi-Fi?

No. It creates a QR code containing your network details. A compatible device can scan it and offer to connect.

Is the QR code safe to share?

The QR code may contain your Wi-Fi password. Anyone who obtains it could potentially access your network.
