Frequently asked questions

Will the image quality change?

PNG is a lossless image format. The conversion preserves the decoded image pixels, though it does not restore detail already lost in the original JPG.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. This tool converts images directly in your browser.

Can I convert PNG images back to JPG here?

This tool converts JPG and JPEG images to PNG. Use the separate PNG to JPG tool for the reverse conversion.
