How to Compress a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Select your preferred compression level.
  3. Click Compress PDF.
  4. Compare the original and new file sizes.
  5. Download your compressed PDF.

Why Can Some PDFs Not Be Compressed Much?

PDFs can contain text, images, fonts, scanned pages and already-compressed content. If a PDF is already optimized, additional compression may provide little or no size reduction.
