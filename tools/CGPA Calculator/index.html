How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated by dividing the sum of each subject's grade point multiplied by its credits by the total credits.

CGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

This calculator uses a 0–10 grade-point scale. Check your institution's grading system before entering your results.
