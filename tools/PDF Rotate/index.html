How to rotate a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Select a rotation angle: 90°, 180° or 270°.
  3. Choose all pages or enter specific page numbers.
  4. Click Rotate PDF and download the updated document.

About this PDF Rotate tool

Correct sideways or upside-down PDF pages before printing, sharing or reading. The tool changes page orientation in the PDF document itself and processes the file in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Choose Selected pages only and enter the page number you want to rotate.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. The tool creates a separate rotated PDF for download. Your original file remains unchanged.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

This tool processes the selected PDF locally in your browser and does not upload it to a UtilityHub server.
