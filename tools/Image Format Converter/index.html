Supported image formats

Common browser-readable formats include JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF and AVIF. Actual support depends on your browser. HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, ICO and animated formats may need additional decoding libraries.

Frequently asked questions

Will every image format work?

Not necessarily. Browser support varies, and some formats require a dedicated decoder. The tool should report when a format cannot be read or exported.

Will transparency be preserved?

PNG and WebP can support transparency. JPG does not, so transparent areas need a background color when converting to JPG.

Are my images uploaded?

The intended conversion process runs locally in your browser. Your selected image should not be sent to a server.
