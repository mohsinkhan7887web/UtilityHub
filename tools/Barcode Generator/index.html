How to use the barcode generator

  1. Enter the text or number you want encoded.
  2. Choose a supported barcode format and width.
  3. Click Generate Barcode to preview it.
  4. Download the PNG and test it with a barcode scanner.

Note: A generated barcode does not automatically provide an official retail product identifier. For retail products, use a valid identifier from the relevant issuing authority.
