How to resize an image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Enter your preferred width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep aspect ratio locked to maintain the original proportions, or unlock it for custom dimensions.
  4. Select an output format and click Resize Image.
  5. Preview and download your resized image.

Frequently asked questions

What is aspect ratio?

Aspect ratio describes the relationship between an image's width and height. Locking it helps prevent unwanted stretching.

Will resizing improve image quality?

Resizing changes image dimensions but cannot restore details that were not present in the original image.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. This tool is designed to resize images locally in your browser.
