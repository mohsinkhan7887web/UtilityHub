Frequently asked questions

Can I delete multiple pages at once?

Yes. Use a comma-separated list such as 2,4,7-9.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. The tool creates a separate PDF and leaves your original file unchanged.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No upload to our server is needed. Processing takes place in your browser.
