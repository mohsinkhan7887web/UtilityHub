Frequently asked questions

Does JPG support transparency?

No. Transparent areas are filled with the background color you select before conversion.

Will image quality change?

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image detail may be lost. A higher quality setting can reduce visible artifacts.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. This tool is designed to process images locally in your browser.
