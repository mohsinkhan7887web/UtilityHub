How does a SIP Calculator work?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount regularly. This calculator estimates the future value assuming a constant monthly rate of return and investments made at the end of each month.

Future Value = P × [((1 + r)n − 1) ÷ r]

Here, P is the monthly investment, r is the estimated monthly return rate, and n is the total number of monthly investments. With a 0% return, the future value equals the total amount invested.
