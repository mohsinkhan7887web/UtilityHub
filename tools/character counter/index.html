What Does a Character Counter Do?

A character counter counts the number of characters in your text, including letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces. It can also count words, lines and sentences.

How to Use the Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the character, word, line and sentence counts instantly.
  3. Click Copy Text to copy your text.
  4. Click Clear to start again.

Privacy

Your text is processed directly in your browser. It is not uploaded to a server.
