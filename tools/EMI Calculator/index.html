EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P = loan principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of monthly payments.

How to use the EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the amount you want to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate offered by the lender.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Click Calculate EMI to estimate your monthly payment.

Note: This is an estimate based on a fixed interest rate and regular monthly payments. Actual lender charges and repayment schedules may differ.
